02 About

GarayTech is a Czech-registered company with two distinct but complementary areas of activity. Our trading practice is built on years of experience in the global commodities market — sourcing, moving, and placing goods across international borders, with a particular focus on natural and specialty ingredients through our sister brand Bulkvanilla.

Our IT consultancy practice is built on 25 years of B2B sales and market entry experience across Central and Eastern Europe, CIS, and Russia. We work with technology companies — particularly US, Israeli, and Western European firms — that need a senior commercial leader on the ground in CEE. On a fractional or interim basis, we open markets, build pipeline, establish partnerships, and where needed, build sales teams from scratch. The engagement ends with a functioning commercial operation, not a slide deck.

We are a small, focused operation. That means you talk to the people who actually do the work.
